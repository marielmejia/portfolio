About

I’m an art director and designer with more than 20 years of agency experience.

I enjoy every part of the process: shaping the creative strategy, getting hands-on with the design, leading design teams and working closely with clients and collaborators from every discipline.

Since 2017, I’ve been a senior art director and designer at 22 Fillmore, a branding and marketing firm in the San Francisco Bay Area. Most of my work there has been in healthcare (biotech, life sciences and medtech), along with technology and financial services. Our websites for Ikena (2020), Aeglea (2021) and Kardigan (2026) each won Best Biotechnology Website of the Year from the Web Marketing Association.

I approach every project with the same goal: create something that looks exceptional and serves a clear purpose. A website should tell a story, show what makes a brand different and guide visitors toward action.

If you like what you see, I’d love to hear from you. Send me an email or connect with me on LinkedIn.
